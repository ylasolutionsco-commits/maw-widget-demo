Privacy notice

Draft for review. YLA Solutions should have this checked by a privacy adviser before the demo call goes live.

The demo call

  • When you ask for a demo call, we use your mobile number to text you a one-time code and then call you once with our AI agent.
  • The call is with an automated AI assistant. You can ask for a person at any time.
  • Calls are not recorded. We keep a short summary and, if you ask us to, your name and the reason you'd like a consultation so we can follow up.
  • We keep your number for 30 days to stop the demo being misused, then delete it, unless you've asked us to contact you.

Who processes it

Twilio (texts and calls), Vapi (the voice assistant), Anthropic (the AI model) and Supabase (our database). Some of these providers may process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing ylasolutionsco@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
