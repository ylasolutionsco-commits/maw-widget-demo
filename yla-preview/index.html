Questions

Commonly asked

What is an AI agent?

Software that talks to your customers on the phone or in chat, in plain English. It answers from information you've approved and does set jobs such as booking appointments.

Can it answer calls 24/7?

Yes. It picks up instantly at any time, including evenings, weekends and when your team is busy.

Can it book straight into my diary?

Yes, with booking and practice systems that allow it. Where yours doesn't, the agent takes the booking request and passes it to your team, and never tells a caller they're booked unless the booking is really made.

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as an automated assistant and customers can ask for a person at any time.

Can it put a caller through to a real person?

Yes. It can transfer the call or pass the details to the right person on your team with a summary.

Will it give medical or professional advice?

No. Questions that need a professional go straight to your team. The agent only says what you've approved.

Does it get better over time?

Yes. We review conversations with you, update the answers you approve and improve how it handles your calls.
